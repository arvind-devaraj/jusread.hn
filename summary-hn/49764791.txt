AI-generated posters don’t have to be horrible  (177 pts, 120 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49764791

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the quality and perception of AI-generated event posters, sparked by an article arguing that these posters don't have to be poorly designed. The author expresses frustration with the uniformity and low effort often associated with AI-generated designs, which have become ubiquitous for various local events.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and debate. Some users acknowledge that while AI-generated posters can lack originality, they often replace even worse designs, such as those made with basic tools like WordArt. Others argue that the AI-generated posters still carry a distinct "AI smell" and lack the creativity and vision that human designers might bring. A few commenters suggest that the saturation of AI-generated content has led to a general desensitization, making them less impressive over time.

Notable insights include the idea that the quality of design is often tied to the effort put in by the creator, whether human or AI. Some commenters also reflect on the historical context of design trends, suggesting that uniformity in design is not a new phenomenon and has existed in various forms throughout history. Overall, the discussion highlights the tension between embracing AI tools for convenience and the desire for unique, high-quality design.
