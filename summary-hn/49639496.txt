Show HN: Botbin.io – pastebin for AI agent artifacts  (10 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49639496

The Hacker News discussion centers around Botbin.io, a platform designed as a "pastebin" for AI agent artifacts, allowing users to store and share data related to AI interactions. The concept is likened to a collaborative whiteboard for AI agents, facilitating better organization and versioning of code and artifacts.

In the comments, users express interest in the platform's potential as a persistence layer for stateless bots, suggesting it could enhance cooperation among them. There are also ideas about adding features like a "just pushed" display for app updates and a request for an MCP endpoint. One commenter humorously suggests the platform could serve as a honeypot, while another mentions owning a related domain name, indicating a broader interest in the topic.

Overall, the discussion highlights enthusiasm for the idea of improving AI agent collaboration and the need for better tools to manage their artifacts, with various suggestions for enhancements and features.
