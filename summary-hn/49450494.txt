OpenAI is "80% of the way" to AGI  (11 pts, 18 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49450494

The Hacker News discussion revolves around an article that reports OpenAI's Chief Research Officer, Mark Chen, claiming the company is "80% of the way" to achieving Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The article explores OpenAI's ambitions and the implications of this milestone for humanity.

In the comments, there is significant skepticism regarding the claim of being so close to AGI. Many users reference the Pareto principle, suggesting that the final 20% of development could be as challenging as the first 80%. Some commenters express concern that OpenAI may be overstating their progress to boost their valuation amid competition with other AI companies. Others highlight ethical dilemmas and potential societal impacts of AGI, questioning the readiness of society for such advancements.

Notable insights include the idea that OpenAI's statements may be strategic, aimed at enhancing their market position rather than reflecting actual readiness for AGI. Additionally, there are discussions about the limitations of current models and the necessity for continuous data processing to achieve true sentience, suggesting that the path to AGI is fraught with complexities that are not easily resolved.
