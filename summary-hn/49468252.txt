Tell HN: Man, AI is killing my brain  (10 pts, 6 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468252

The Hacker News discussion centers around a user's experience with AI, specifically Claude Code, and its impact on their cognitive abilities and coding skills. Initially resistant to using AI, the user began to rely on it to keep up with colleagues, leading to a decline in their ability to think critically and write code independently. They express concern about becoming overly dependent on AI, feeling like a "mindless blob" as their mental capacity diminishes.

In the comments, several users resonate with the original poster's feelings, sharing similar experiences of cognitive atrophy due to AI reliance. Some suggest that AI might be detrimental to mental acuity, while others propose that using AI can be seen as delegating tasks, allowing for more focus on creative problem-solving. Notable insights include the idea that while AI can enhance productivity, it may also lead to a loss of fundamental skills and critical thinking, prompting discussions about finding a balance between leveraging AI and maintaining one's cognitive abilities.
