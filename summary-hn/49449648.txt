Show HN: How much of Hacker News is about AI?  (67 pts, 40 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49449648

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a website that analyzes the prevalence of AI-related content on Hacker News (HN). The site tracks titles containing the word "AI" and provides statistics showing that 14.4% of new titles this year are AI-related, a notable increase from previous years. The discussion highlights trends over time, with significant growth in AI-related posts since 2016, and a peak of 15.9% in February 2026.

In the comments, users express interest in further categorizing posts, with suggestions for using embeddings to create visual representations of content types. There is also debate about the extent to which posts are written by AI, with some users sharing experiences of detecting AI-generated content and discussing the reliability of AI detection tools. Notably, there is a distinction made between posts about AI and those generated by AI, with some commenters advocating for tracking AI-generated comments as well.

Overall, the discussion reflects a growing curiosity about the role of AI in the HN community, with users eager to explore the implications of AI-generated content and its impact on discussions.
