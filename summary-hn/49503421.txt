Show HN: Academa – Long-form STEM lecture videos generated by LLMs  (30 pts, 25 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49503421

The Hacker News discussion centers around Academa, a platform created by PhD students Sina Atalay and Abdullah Geduk, which generates long-form STEM lecture videos using large language models (LLMs). Their innovative approach treats lecture content as code, allowing for continuous updates and corrections, unlike traditional recorded lectures that remain static. Each video is accompanied by an AI chat feature that interacts with viewers based on the lecture content.

In the comments, users express both enthusiasm and skepticism. Some appreciate the "videos as code" concept for its potential to iterate on educational content and cater to different learning styles. However, others raise concerns about the accuracy and delivery of the generated lectures, noting issues like misplaced emphasis and abrupt transitions. There is also debate about the value of video lectures compared to traditional teaching methods, with some arguing that direct interaction with human instructors is irreplaceable.

Notable insights include the recognition of the economic advantages of Academa's model, as it avoids the high costs associated with traditional video production. Additionally, some commenters highlight the importance of user experience and the need for improvements in the quality of the generated content to compete with established educational platforms. Overall, while the concept is seen as promising, there are significant concerns regarding execution and the inherent limitations of LLMs in educational contexts.
