Ask HN: What have you been curious about?  (10 pts, 20 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49101448

The Hacker News discussion titled "Ask HN: What have you been curious about?" invites users to reflect on their past curiosities rather than current interests, emphasizing the value of looking back to inform future pursuits. The original poster suggests that this retrospective approach can reconnect individuals with their innate curiosity, while also requesting that discussions avoid the prevalent topic of large language models (LLMs).

In the comments, there is a consensus on the richness of curiosity, with users sharing diverse topics ranging from the training data of AI models to personal interests like art, literature, and geopolitical shifts. Notable insights include a discussion about the transparency of AI training data, reflections on the complexity of the human brain compared to computers, and the exploration of historical and cultural subjects, such as Dr. King's writing and the dynamics of energy markets. The conversation highlights the varied nature of curiosity and the personal projects that stem from it, reinforcing the idea that curiosity can be fulfilling in itself, regardless of practical outcomes.
