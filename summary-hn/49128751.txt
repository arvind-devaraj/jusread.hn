Thomson Reuters built its own AI model that now ranks among the best  (18 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49128751

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a post by Thomson Reuters announcing that they have developed an AI model that ranks among the best in the world. The article highlights the model's capabilities, particularly in legal research, and suggests it has significant advantages over existing models.

In the comments, users express skepticism about the claims made in the press release. Some point out that the company needs to persuade traditional law firm partners of its value rather than the general public. Others note that the comparisons made in the release lack rigor, as they involve testing against older models while using proprietary legal data, which may not provide a fair assessment against models trained on publicly available data. There is a general consensus that the announcement may be more about marketing than substantive innovation.

Notable opinions include concerns about the lack of transparency in the benchmarking process and the potential for the model to be overhyped. Some commenters appreciate the effort to innovate but remain critical of the press release's claims, suggesting that a more thorough and objective evaluation is necessary to validate the model's performance.
