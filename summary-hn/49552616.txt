Launch HN: Mireye (YC S26) – Infrastructure for Physical World AI Agents  (28 pts, 6 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49552616

The Hacker News discussion centers around Mireye, a startup founded by Ansh Chokshi that provides infrastructure for AI agents to make informed decisions about physical locations in the U.S. Mireye offers a comprehensive API that combines data, enrichment, tools, and signals for any U.S. location, addressing the limitations of existing AI models that struggle with specific geographic queries. Chokshi shares insights from his experience building construction agents and highlights the challenges of gathering and normalizing data from various sources.

In the comments, users express appreciation for Mireye's approach, particularly its method of handling data gaps by returning statuses like "ok," "absent," or "failed" instead of ambiguous null values. There is a discussion about the need for clarity in the pitch, especially for those in the AI space who may associate such services with low-value projects. Commenters also emphasize the importance of accurately interpreting data from different jurisdictions, noting that the meaning of null can vary significantly.

Overall, the discussion reflects a strong interest in Mireye's potential applications across various industries, including insurance, proptech, and urban planning, while also raising important considerations about data accuracy and the challenges of building reliable AI systems for real-world applications.
