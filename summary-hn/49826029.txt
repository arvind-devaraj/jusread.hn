Ask HN: I just talked to an AI-obsessed client, and I need a shower afterwards  (12 pts, 10 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49826029

The Hacker News discussion centers around a freelancer's unsettling conversation with a client who is heavily invested in AI tools for creative tasks, such as automated marketing and content generation. The freelancer expresses concern that the ease of access to these technologies is undermining the value of traditional skills and creativity, leading to a "dead internet" filled with low-quality content. They feel that their lifelong passions are being threatened by newcomers who can now engage in creative endeavors with minimal effort.

In the comments, there is a consensus on the anxiety surrounding the proliferation of AI in creative fields. Many users share similar feelings of disillusionment, with some opting to disconnect from technology or return to more traditional learning methods. Others highlight the cyclical nature of industries, suggesting that while the cost of producing content has decreased, the importance of taste and originality remains crucial. Notable insights include the idea that good taste may become a key differentiator in a landscape flooded with easily generated content, and a recognition that the current era may be viewed negatively in hindsight, particularly for those not benefiting from the AI boom.
