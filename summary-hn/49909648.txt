Show HN: Lathoa, a math app for kids where the AI is wrong on purpose  (52 pts, 47 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49909648

The Hacker News discussion centers around Lathoa, a math app designed for children aged 10 to 14, where an AI named Errol intentionally makes mistakes in solving math problems. The app encourages kids to identify these errors and explain why they are wrong, promoting critical thinking skills. Users earn experience points (XP) for finding mistakes and providing explanations, with a leaderboard feature available for parents to enable.

In the comments, users debate the educational value of the app, particularly whether identifying errors teaches more than solving problems independently. Some believe it fosters critical thinking, while others express concerns about the potential for rushing through problems due to the speed component of the game. There are also discussions about the app's reliance on AI and its presentation, with some users suggesting that it feels overly automated or lacks a personal touch.

Notable insights include the recognition that teaching kids to question information is valuable, and the acknowledgment that the app's design may inadvertently encourage a style of thinking similar to that of AI. Additionally, there are concerns about GDPR compliance and the app's data handling practices. Overall, the discussion reflects a mix of enthusiasm for the app's concept and critical examination of its implementation and educational efficacy.
