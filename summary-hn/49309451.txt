Working with AI Feels More Like Leadership Than Coding  (181 pts, 124 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49309451

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the idea that working with AI, particularly large language models (LLMs), resembles leadership more than traditional coding. The author suggests that successfully leveraging AI requires skills akin to managing people, as it involves guiding and influencing the AI's outputs rather than merely writing code.

In the comments, there is a debate about the validity of this comparison. Some users agree, noting that individuals with strong people management skills often achieve better results with AI. Others argue that deep technical knowledge remains crucial and that the traditional coding mindset still applies, regardless of AI's capabilities. There are differing opinions on the definitions of "leadership" and "management," with some asserting that leadership is about influencing others, while management is about overseeing tasks or projects.

Notable insights include the idea that LLMs can be seen as tools that require a structured approach to yield effective results, similar to managing human teams. Some commenters express skepticism about the notion that leadership skills are transferable to AI management, emphasizing the unique challenges posed by working with non-human agents. Overall, the discussion highlights the evolving nature of software development in the age of AI and the ongoing debate about the roles of leadership and technical expertise in this context.
