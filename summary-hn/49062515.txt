Ask HN: How do you figure out what you're passionate about?  (12 pts, 21 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49062515

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a user who has spent 15 years in the tech industry and is now struggling to identify their passions after experiencing burnout. They express feelings of exhaustion and a lack of energy to explore new interests, leading them to seek advice on how to rediscover what they are passionate about.

In the comments, several users resonate with the original poster's situation, sharing their own experiences of burnout and the challenges of finding new interests. Common suggestions include taking a break to reflect, engaging in physical activity, talking to others to discover new hobbies, and experimenting with various activities to see what resonates. Some commenters emphasize that passion may develop over time through mastery rather than being an innate trait to be found.

Notable insights include the idea that passion can be a misleading concept, as it often involves suffering and hard work. Others suggest focusing on being useful to others as a way to cultivate passion, while some propose journaling or seeking professional guidance to clarify one's interests. Overall, the discussion highlights the complexities of passion and the importance of self-reflection and exploration in the journey to rediscover it.
