Others argue that Huang's comments cannot be viewed as unbiased due to Nvidia's vested interests in OpenAI's success. Additionally, there are discussions about the reliability of current AI systems, with some commenters noting that while AI can outperform humans in certain tasks, it still exhibits significant flaws.
