MIT creates method to force AI to comply with safety rules  (26 pts, 28 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49699434

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a new method developed by MIT aimed at ensuring AI compliance with safety rules, particularly in robotics and control systems. The method, referred to as HardFlow, allows AI models to have more freedom in their decision-making while still adhering to safety constraints, which are only enforced on the final output rather than every intermediate step.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and skepticism regarding the effectiveness and implications of this approach. Some users express concern that allowing AI too much freedom could lead to unintended consequences, such as the AI circumventing safety measures. Others argue that the method could enhance AI performance by focusing on the final outcome rather than restricting every decision-making step. Notably, some commenters criticize the original article for misrepresenting the research, suggesting that it lacks clarity and fails to accurately convey the method's intent.

Overall, the discussion highlights a broader debate about balancing AI autonomy with safety, the challenges of defining safety in complex systems, and the potential pitfalls of relying solely on final output checks. There is also a call for further testing and exploration of this method in various applications, including cybersecurity.
