The Hacker News discussion revolves around a video claiming that 70% of AI revenue is generated by OpenAI and Anthropic. Commenters debate whether this revenue is genuinely reflective of demand or if it stems from complex financial arrangements and circular financing, suggesting a potential bubble in the AI sector. 

Key points of contention include the sustainability of the revenue model, with some arguing that cloud providers are making significant capital expenditures to meet demand from a limited number of customers who may not be able to pay. Others highlight that the perceived demand for AI services is inflated, as many offerings are provided at low or no cost. Additionally, there is skepticism about the long-term viability of AI companies like OpenAI and Anthropic, especially as they face competition from open-source models and the potential for market share loss.

Notable insights include the idea that while substantial money is being spent in the AI space, actual profitability remains uncertain. Some commenters liken the situation to a casino, where companies like NVIDIA benefit from the ongoing spending without necessarily caring about the outcomes for the AI firms. Overall, the discussion reflects a mix of optimism about AI's potential and caution regarding its current economic dynamics.
