What would a serious AI product look like?  (124 pts, 52 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49876148

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the concept of what constitutes a "serious AI product." The original post critiques the current state of AI applications, particularly large language models (LLMs), suggesting that many existing products lack transparency and reliability. It emphasizes the need for better user experience, including improved data handling and user control over personal information.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and debate. Some users argue that many consumer-facing AI products are not serious and are often poorly integrated, while others defend the current offerings, asserting that improvements are indeed possible without dismissing existing technologies. A recurring theme is the tension between user desires for data privacy and control versus corporate interests in data monetization. Notable insights include calls for local-first data storage options and the idea that AI should not be marketed as a standalone product but rather as an integrated tool that enhances existing workflows.

Overall, the discussion highlights the complexities of developing AI products that genuinely meet user needs while navigating ethical and practical challenges.
