LLMs as a Cognitive Virus  (281 pts, 198 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580164

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a paper titled "LLMs as a Cognitive Virus," which explores the social and cognitive implications of large language models (LLMs). The authors use the term "cognitive virus" not in a negative sense, but to describe how LLMs can influence human thought and behavior, akin to how viruses affect their hosts. This raises questions about the societal impact of LLMs and the need for interdisciplinary perspectives in understanding their effects.

In the comments, participants debate the appropriateness of the "virus" analogy, with some suggesting "contagion" or "STD outbreak" as more fitting terms. There is a consensus on the importance of including diverse viewpoints—both technical and non-technical—in discussions about technology's societal impacts. Some commenters express concern that LLMs can be both beneficial and detrimental, serving as tools for productivity while also potentially leading to cognitive dependency or societal issues.

Notable insights include the idea that LLMs may alter human cognition and communication, with some users finding their outputs increasingly tailored to other LLMs rather than being human-readable. Others reflect on the cultural and emotional responses to LLMs, likening their adoption to religious fervor or cult-like behavior. Overall, the discussion highlights a mix of enthusiasm and skepticism regarding the future of LLMs and their role in society.
