The post highlights the benefits of these updates, particularly for free users who can now utilize Luna without limits.

In the comments, users express mixed opinions about the different models—Luna, Sol, and Terra. Others criticize the engagement-driven responses of the models, suggesting that they prioritize user engagement over providing direct answers.
