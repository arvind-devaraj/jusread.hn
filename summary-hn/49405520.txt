Why can AI generate Super Mario but not a wedge ramp for my robot vacuum?  (11 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49405520

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the author's frustration with AI's inability to generate functional 3D models, like a wedge ramp for a robot vacuum, compared to its success in creating aesthetic models, such as figurines or characters. The author experimented with various AI tools but found them inadequate for practical design tasks. Ultimately, they discovered that breaking down complex designs into smaller, manageable specifications and using code execution in Blender yielded better results. They posed questions about the disparity in AI's performance between functional and aesthetic generation and sought insights on improving the modeling process.

In the comments, users generally agree that parametric design tools, like OpenSCAD, can yield better results for functional parts compared to traditional modeling approaches. Several commenters shared their successes using AI to generate CAD designs, emphasizing the importance of structured prompts and the potential of using code for design. Notably, one user pointed out that the difficulty lies in the need for a geometric modeling kernel to create BRep CAD models, suggesting that the challenges are more technical than related to training data. Overall, the discussion highlights the ongoing challenges and potential solutions in leveraging AI for practical 3D modeling tasks.
