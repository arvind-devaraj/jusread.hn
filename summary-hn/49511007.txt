Launch HN: Almanac (YC S26) – AI that knows your company  (40 pts, 36 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49511007

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the launch of Almanac, an AI tool designed to serve as a personal and company knowledge agent. Founded by Kushagra and his co-founders, Almanac aims to streamline information management by integrating various accounts (like Gmail and Calendar) and organizing data into two wikis: a personal one for individual use and a company-wide one. The tool emphasizes proactive task management and long-term project tracking, distinguishing itself from other AI assistants by focusing on a pre-compiled knowledge base that enhances memory and context.

In the comments, users express concerns about the 7-day trial period being too short for a product that relies on building memory over time. There are also discussions about privacy, security, and the handling of permissions within the wikis. Some commenters question how Almanac differentiates itself from existing competitors and whether it can effectively manage tasks without violating terms of service for connected tools. Notably, Kushagra responds to these concerns by highlighting the unique capabilities of Almanac's wiki layer and its proactive features, while also acknowledging the competitive landscape and the need for clearer communication about the product's benefits.
