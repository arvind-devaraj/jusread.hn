Show HN: Real-time AI news aggregator with daily digest  (10 pts, 14 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550515

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a new project titled "AI Briefs," a real-time news aggregator focused on AI-related content, which also offers a daily digest. The creator, michelmi, is actively seeking feedback and suggestions for improving the site.

Commenters generally express enthusiasm for the concept, with many highlighting its utility for staying updated on AI news. Suggestions for enhancements include increasing information density, adding features like RSS feeds, implementing a masonry grid layout, and creating a minimalist theme. Some users critique the current UI design, suggesting it resembles other platforms and could be simplified for better readability. Notably, michelmi acknowledges these critiques and indicates a willingness to iterate on the design based on user feedback. Additionally, there is interest in the underlying technology used for summarization, with michelmi mentioning the use of specific AI models for generating content. Overall, the discussion reflects a collaborative spirit aimed at refining the aggregator's functionality and user experience.
