It’s so hard to finish an idea that is not yours and is just suggested by AI  (120 pts, 70 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49450898

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the challenges of completing ideas generated by AI, particularly in the context of coding and note-taking. The original post highlights the difficulty of finishing AI-suggested ideas, as they often lack personal ownership and depth. 

In the comments, several users express a shared struggle with both AI-generated and personal ideas, emphasizing that AI can produce misleading or "hallucinated" information that complicates the development process. There is a debate about the role of comments in code, with many users advocating for minimal or no comments from AI, as they can introduce confusion and hinder future modifications. Some suggest establishing clear specifications and guidelines for AI interactions to mitigate these issues.

Notable insights include the idea that AI should maintain its own notes separate from human-generated content to avoid confusion and ensure clarity. Users also discuss the importance of provenance in notes to distinguish between AI-generated and human decisions, highlighting the need for careful curation of context when working with AI tools. Overall, the conversation reflects a broader concern about the integration of AI in creative and technical processes, emphasizing the need for clear boundaries and thoughtful management.
