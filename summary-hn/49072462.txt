Show HN: FeyNoBg – Automatic background removal model and training library  (47 pts, 13 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49072462

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the release of FeyNoBg, an automatic background removal model developed by Feyn, along with an open-source Python library called NoBg for training and running the model. The model aims to improve the accuracy of foreground identification and boundary precision in images, addressing challenges such as camouflage and fine structures like hair. The creators emphasize their interpretability-first approach to training, which has led to impressive benchmark results.

In the comments, users express enthusiasm for the tool, with some discussing its resolution capabilities and ease of use compared to other tools. There is a notable exchange about licensing, with one user questioning the decision to extend the weights of an MIT-licensed model (BiRefNet) under a different license (cc-by-nc-4.0). Additionally, a user shares their interest in porting the model to mobile devices, prompting a positive response from the developers, who encourage collaboration.

Overall, the discussion highlights excitement about advancements in background removal technology, the importance of open-source tools in this space, and some concerns regarding licensing and compatibility.
