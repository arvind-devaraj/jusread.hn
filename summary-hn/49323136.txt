Red queen hypothesis – A new way forward for self-improving AI  (47 pts, 10 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49323136

The Hacker News discussion centers around a new approach to self-improving AI based on the Red Queen hypothesis, which suggests that both the AI agent and its evaluator should evolve together to enhance performance. This method aims to address limitations of traditional AI training, where agents improve against a static evaluator. The article highlights the collaborative evolution of agents and evaluators as a potential breakthrough in AI development.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and skepticism. Some users draw parallels between this new method and concepts from the 1990s, such as co-evolving systems and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Others express concerns about the practical limitations of this approach, suggesting it may only be effective for problems already defined by humans. Additionally, there is a discussion about public perception of AI, with some commenters noting a general sense of distress rather than excitement about AI advancements.

Notable insights include the recognition that the method may not be as novel as presented, as similar ideas were explored in earlier evolutionary computing research. There is also a call for more emphasis on the implications of evolving evaluators alongside agents, which could significantly impact the effectiveness of self-improvement in AI.
