Show HN: Boffin – Staff-engineer layer for AI coding agents  (15 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49060279

The Hacker News discussion centers around a project called Boffin, which serves as a control layer for AI coding agents. The author, mic_sm, explains that Boffin addresses a common issue where AI agents produce overly extensive code changes instead of the requested small fixes. By providing architectural constraints specific to the file being edited, Boffin aims to ensure that the AI's modifications are more relevant and manageable.

In the comments, there is a mix of skepticism and interest. User johnfn emphasizes the importance of a robust evaluation suite to validate the effectiveness of such projects, suggesting that a single successful edit is insufficient evidence of reliability. PufPufPuf questions the relevance of the specific line count in the project's headline case, while capeavace expresses a desire for collaboration on improving quality verification methods for coding agents. Supermancho notes that Boffin adds contextual guidance for AI agents, akin to traditional documentation practices.

Overall, the discussion highlights both the potential benefits of Boffin in improving AI coding efficiency and the challenges of establishing credibility and effectiveness in AI-driven coding solutions.
