Show HN: Give your AI agent on-screen guides that show users where to click  (11 pts, 7 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49627872

The Hacker News discussion centers around a new tool called Assist API, developed by Christian and his team at Frigade, which aims to enhance in-app AI agents by providing on-screen guides for users. The tool addresses the common issue where AI agents struggle to understand the specific functionalities of the software they are integrated with. Instead of relying on outdated help center articles or generic internet searches, the Assist API gathers context from the user's interface and generates tailored guides or explanations based on the user's queries.

In the comments, there is a consensus on the potential of the tool to not only assist users but also to identify gaps in the product's UI that could be improved. Some users express skepticism about adding complexity to software rather than simplifying it, while others acknowledge the importance of addressing user experience issues through AI insights. Notably, one commenter highlights the risk of AI complicating well-designed applications, emphasizing the need for a balance between AI integration and software usability. Overall, the discussion reflects a mix of enthusiasm for the tool's capabilities and concern about the broader implications of AI in software development.
