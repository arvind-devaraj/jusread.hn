Show HN: 1667, a terminal UI for writing fiction with language models  (32 pts, 87 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49330604

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a project called 1667, a terminal-based UI designed for writing fiction using language models. The creator, who built the tool for personal use, prefers terminal interfaces over web UIs and aims to facilitate a branching narrative style that reflects their writing process. The application allows users to manage multiple takes on story segments, export them to Markdown, and supports various AI providers for generating text.

In the comments, users express a mix of admiration for the tool's design and skepticism about the implications of using AI in creative writing. Some appreciate the unique approach and potential for niche storytelling, while others worry that reliance on AI could diminish genuine creativity and imagination, especially in younger generations. Notable insights include the idea that tools like 1667 can enhance creativity rather than replace it, and the acknowledgment that every new technology carries both benefits and drawbacks. The creator also emphasizes that the tool is not intended to replace traditional writing but to serve as an additional resource for exploring storytelling.
