Ask HN: What are the most promising RL fields for a new master student?  (30 pts, 12 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49057972

The Hacker News discussion centers around an incoming master's student seeking advice on promising research directions in reinforcement learning (RL), particularly in the areas of embodied AI and brain-computer interfaces (BCIs). 

Commenters generally agree that focusing on practical applications, such as closed-loop adaptive BCIs and sim-to-real robotics, is a strong direction. Several users emphasize the importance of aligning research interests with the expertise of assistant professors at the student's institution, as they are often more eager to collaborate and publish. There is a consensus that choosing a topic that genuinely captivates the student is crucial for long-term engagement and success. Additionally, some commenters suggest exploring emerging areas like intrinsic motivation in RL, which could lead to significant breakthroughs.

Notable insights include the recommendation to leverage existing projects and ideas from faculty, as well as the importance of considering available resources and funding when selecting a research topic. Overall, the discussion highlights the balance between personal interest and practical feasibility in choosing a research path in RL.
