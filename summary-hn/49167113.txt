In the comments, there is a debate about the implications of AI-generated content. There’s a consensus that AI-generated work should be clearly marked to allow readers to make informed choices. Notably, some commenters argue that public shaming of creators using AI is counterproductive, advocating instead for a more constructive approach, such as simply ignoring content that doesn't meet personal standards.

Overall, the discussion reflects a broader concern about the impact of AI on creativity and originality in content creation, with varying opinions on how to address the issue.
