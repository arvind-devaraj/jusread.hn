Show HN: Give your AI agents access to WhatsApp  (14 pts, 31 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49728159

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a project called Chat-Man, which allows users to give AI agents access to WhatsApp without needing to integrate a separate WhatsApp library for each project. The tool provides a WhatsApp MCP server that enables users to read, send, and manage messages, as well as extract information and manage group memberships through a web UI. The creator, Fabian, built this tool for personal projects and is seeking feedback, particularly from those who need WhatsApp access for their agents.

In the comments, there is significant debate about the potential risks and ethical implications of using such a tool. Many users express concerns about the possibility of violating WhatsApp's terms of service (ToS), which could lead to account bans. Some commenters argue that while the tool could enhance efficiency for small businesses, it poses risks of spam and data privacy violations. Others question the security of the tool, particularly regarding how it handles sensitive data. Notably, there is skepticism about the long-term viability of using such tools without facing repercussions from WhatsApp. Overall, the discussion highlights a tension between the utility of the tool and the ethical and legal considerations surrounding its use.
