ArXiv has almost 600 submissions today and most are AI slop  (22 pts, 15 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49517928

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a post highlighting the influx of nearly 600 submissions to ArXiv in a single day, with many commenters expressing concern that a significant portion of these submissions consists of low-quality work generated with the help of AI, referred to as "AI slop." The original post suggests that the quality of academic submissions may be deteriorating due to the ease of producing content with AI tools.

In the comments, there is a debate about whether AI-generated content can be considered "slop." Some argue that AI can be a useful tool for expressing complex ideas if properly reviewed, while others contend that the sheer volume of submissions makes it difficult to discern valuable work from low-quality output. Concerns are raised about the declining signal-to-noise ratio in academic publishing, with some commenters lamenting the state of academic writing and the pressures of publishing in a system that often prioritizes quantity over quality.

Notable insights include the acknowledgment that the crisis in academic publishing predates AI and stems from systemic issues, such as the demand for published papers in graduate programs leading to a proliferation of mediocre research. There is a shared worry that the rise of AI-generated content could further undermine confidence in academic publishing and exacerbate existing problems in the field.
