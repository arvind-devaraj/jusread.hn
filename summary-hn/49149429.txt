The diabolical world of convincing AI thirst traps  (15 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49149429

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a Vox article that explores the phenomenon of AI-generated "thirst traps" on social media platforms like TikTok and Instagram. These AI-generated images and videos often feature attractive influencers that may not exist in reality, raising questions about authenticity and the impact of deepfake technology on social media culture.

In the comments, users express a mix of fascination and skepticism regarding the effectiveness of these AI creations. Some note that while many people engage with these fake influencers, the images often still exhibit telltale signs of being artificial, such as unnatural lighting. There is a debate about the distinction between fully AI-generated content and heavily filtered real images, with some commenters questioning where the line is drawn between "real" and "fake."

Notably, the discussion highlights the ongoing challenge of the "uncanny valley," where AI-generated images can appear almost lifelike but still evoke discomfort. Users also reflect on the broader implications of this technology, considering how it blurs the lines of reality and authenticity in digital interactions.
