The Hacker News discussion titled "AI;DR (AI; Didn't Read)" revolves around the challenges and implications of AI-generated text, particularly in the context of communication and information clarity. The original post likely critiques the verbosity and lack of precision often found in AI-generated content, suggesting that it can obscure important information.

In the comments, users express a range of opinions on the effectiveness and utility of AI-generated text. Many agree that AI outputs can be low-effort and often lack the necessary context or clarity, making them difficult to engage with. Some commenters emphasize the importance of providing the original prompts alongside AI responses to enhance understanding, while others share personal experiences of dealing with overly verbose AI-generated content in professional settings, which can lead to confusion and missed details.

Notable insights include the idea that the quality of AI-generated text should be proportional to the clarity of the human input, and that excessive verbosity can hinder effective communication. Overall, the discussion highlights a growing concern about the balance between AI assistance and the need for clear, concise human communication.
