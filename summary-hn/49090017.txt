FBI Seeks AI for Political Watch List  (21 pts, 5 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49090017

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a report that the FBI is seeking to develop an AI system for creating a political watch list. This initiative raises concerns about the implications of monitoring individuals based on broad and vague criteria, such as "anti-Americanism" and "extremism on migration, race, and gender."

In the comments, users express significant skepticism about the FBI's approach. Many highlight the overly broad definitions of threats, questioning how they relate to actual terrorism or criminal activity. There is a strong sentiment that such a watch list could undermine democratic principles and may even be unconstitutional. Commenters also express concern about the potential misuse of AI in this context, suggesting that it could lead to preemptive actions against individuals based on their political beliefs rather than any concrete evidence of wrongdoing.

Overall, the discussion reflects a deep unease about the intersection of national security, civil liberties, and the role of technology in monitoring political dissent.
