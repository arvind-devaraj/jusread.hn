Show HN: Selfship.ai – Surface and fix isues with your agentic applications 24x7  (10 pts, 9 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49522967

The Hacker News discussion centers around Selfship.ai, a newly launched SaaS product designed to autonomously identify and resolve issues in AI-driven applications, particularly those involving conversational agents. The creators, who have been developing an AI chat-based trading system for three years, found that problems were often only discovered when users reported them. Selfship.ai aims to continuously monitor interactions, categorize failures by user intent, and implement fixes automatically, while also evaluating the effectiveness of those fixes.

In the comments, users express interest in the product's applicability across different domains, with the creators noting early deployments in AI advertising, eCommerce customer support, and their trading system. There is curiosity about the potential for on-premise deployment, which the creators are exploring with specific clients. Key questions arise regarding the accuracy of the fixes proposed by Selfship.ai, with commenters inquiring about the system's ability to differentiate between genuine errors and user drop-offs, as well as the frequency with which engineers merge the generated pull requests. Overall, the discussion highlights both excitement about the technology and skepticism regarding its reliability and adaptability in various contexts.
