FTC chair suggests AI developers should be liable for conduct of agents  (51 pts, 16 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49850999

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a statement from the FTC chair suggesting that AI developers should be held liable for the actions of their AI agents, similar to how manufacturers are responsible for their tools. The conversation highlights the complexities of accountability in AI usage, emphasizing that while AI cannot be held accountable in the same way humans can, developers and users should share responsibility for its actions.

Commenters generally agree that both AI developers and users should be liable, drawing parallels to traditional tools like weed whackers, where liability depends on how the tool is used. Some argue that users should be held accountable for crimes committed by AI on their behalf, while others emphasize the need for manufacturers to ensure the safety and reliability of their AI systems. Notably, there is a call to avoid anthropomorphizing AI, treating it strictly as a tool rather than an independent actor. Concerns about the potential for misuse and the need for regulatory oversight are also raised, reflecting a broader debate about the implications of AI in society.
