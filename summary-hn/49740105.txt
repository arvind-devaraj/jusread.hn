Show HN: Share your AI Setup, Learn from others  (151 pts, 77 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49740105

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a new platform called "mysetup.ai," where users can share their AI setups and learn from each other's configurations and tools. The creator of the site expressed a desire to understand how engineers utilize AI in their workflows, focusing on the tools and methods they employ rather than just the projects they build.

In the comments, users generally appreciate the idea but express concerns about the platform's reliance on a specific connection method (MCP), which some find cumbersome or insecure. There is a debate about the complexity of setups, with some commenters suggesting that simpler, text-based sharing would be more effective. Others highlight the rapid evolution of tools in the AI space, questioning the longevity and relevance of shared setups. Notable insights include the importance of security in AI setups, as mentioned by one user who uses Tailscale to protect sensitive information, and the acknowledgment that many setups can quickly become outdated due to the fast-paced nature of AI development. Overall, while the concept is well-received, there are calls for improvements in usability and flexibility in how users can contribute their setups.
