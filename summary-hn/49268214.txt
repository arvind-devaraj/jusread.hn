The post titled "I'm Done Using AI" discusses the author's decision to stop using AI tools due to negative personal experiences, including feelings of laziness, detachment, and depression. The author argues that reliance on AI has diminished their engagement and understanding of their work, leading to a lack of personal ownership and growth.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and debate. Some users echo the author's sentiments, suggesting that AI can lead to over-reliance and a decline in critical thinking. Others argue that the issues stem more from management's unrealistic expectations of AI and people's inherent laziness rather than the technology itself. Notably, some commenters emphasize that AI can be a useful tool when used appropriately, while others caution against treating AI as a substitute for human effort and creativity.

Overall, the discussion highlights a broader concern about the impact of AI on productivity and personal well-being, with varying opinions on how to balance its use without compromising individual skills and mental health.
