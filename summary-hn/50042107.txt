AI is creating a 'human premium' for art created by people  (69 pts, 113 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50042107

The Hacker News discussion revolves around the concept of a "human premium" in art, particularly in the context of AI-generated works. The original post highlights how AI is flooding the market with art that lacks the personal touch and cultural significance traditionally associated with human-created art. This has led to a growing appreciation for human-made art, which many believe carries more emotional depth and authenticity.

In the comments, there is a mix of agreement and debate. Some users express concern over the quality and originality of AI-generated art, suggesting that much of it is derivative and lacks "soul." Others argue that art has always been influenced by cultural trends and that AI is simply another tool for artists. There are discussions about the ethical implications of using AI in creative fields, with some commenters sharing experiences of artists misrepresenting their use of AI in their work.

Notable insights include the idea that the value of art is often tied to its creator's context and intention, and that AI-generated art may struggle to resonate on a deeper level with audiences. Some users also point out that the distinction between art and entertainment is blurred, with AI potentially lowering the barrier for producing content, but raising questions about the quality and meaning behind it. Overall, the conversation reflects a complex interplay between technology, creativity, and the evolving definition of art.
