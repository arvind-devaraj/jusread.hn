My personal AI benchmark: “Generate an SVG of a frog with a Habsburg jaw”  (141 pts, 75 comments)
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49147622

The Hacker News discussion revolves around a personal AI benchmark created by a user, where they prompt various AI models to "Generate an SVG of a frog with a Habsburg jaw." The Habsburg jaw, a genetic condition characterized by a protruding lower jaw, is humorously tied to the royal lineage known for inbreeding. The benchmark aims to evaluate how well different AI models interpret and execute this specific prompt.

In the comments, users debate the effectiveness of the models in capturing the essence of the prompt. Some commenters note that several models incorporated the concept of royalty into their interpretations, which sparked discussions about whether such extrapolation was appropriate. Others pointed out that while some models produced decent frog faces, they struggled to connect the anatomical feature of the Habsburg jaw to the frog, leading to humorous and unexpected results. There is also curiosity about how AI models approach SVG generation, with some suggesting that it requires a more complex reasoning process compared to generating raster images.

Notable insights include the recognition that the benchmark serves as a strong test for AI creativity, with one model, Opus 5, receiving particular praise for its output. The discussion highlights the challenges AI faces in understanding and executing nuanced artistic prompts, as well as the humorous potential of combining seemingly unrelated concepts in creative tasks.
